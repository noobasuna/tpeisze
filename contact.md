## Feel free to reach out!

If you are interested in **agentic AI safety**, particularly safety and harmfulness in **human relationships and social interactions**, feel free to get in touch. I’m always happy to connect with researchers and students working on related topics.

You can reach me at **tpeisze[at]nii.ac.jp**.

### Interested in an internship at NII?

The **National Institute of Informatics (NII)** in Tokyo, Japan, offers international internship opportunities for Master’s and PhD students from NII partner institutions worldwide.

If you are interested in working on related research, please check the [**NII International Internship Program**](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/about/international/mouresearch/) for the latest eligibility requirements, application procedures, and available research topics.
